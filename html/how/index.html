HOW IT WORKS

One photo, one tap. Your knife gets a birth-card record.

The one shot

Box + kit + birth card — together

Everything in one frame, like this Large Sebenza 31 Annual: the box (label visible), the birth card face-up and readable, the knife, pouch and papers. The card is the star — this is the photo the decoder reads.

Want a prettier register page? Add up to five more shots — the knife open (blade side), closed (handle side), and any details you like (clip, tang stamp, inlay). Pick one as the cover — that's the photo your public page shows, and the closed HERO shot makes a great one. But one good flat-lay is all it takes.

A good card photo

  • Flat, even light — window light beats lamp light
  • Card fills a good chunk of the frame and every printed line is readable
  • Box label (SKU sticker) visible in the same shot
  • Glare across the card — tilt it until the shine is gone
  • No card? That's fine — say so in the note and the box label carries the date. Or print a blank birth card, fill it in by hand, and shoot it with the knife

The process

  1. Sign in

    Email address → tap the link we send you. No passwords.

  2. ADD A KNIFE

    You get a tag (K01, K02 …). One knife at a time, start to finish.

  3. Add your photo

    Take it now or pick from your camera roll — one flat-lay does it. Up to five more (knife open / closed, details) if you like — pick the best as the cover.

  4. Note anything the card can't say

    Optional: where you got it, "no card", left-handed, a special edition story. No maker's card at all? Print a blank one and write what you know.

  5. Hit PROCESS ⚡

    ~15–30 seconds. It reads the birth card and box label and fills in the record — steel, date, inlay, the lot.

  6. Check the card, then SAVE TO REGISTER

    Not right? Save anyway — every field is editable in your register. It saves private unless you tick “show it on my public page”.

  7. Yours to control

    Every knife starts private. Each one has a public toggle, sale status, and a delete. Your private notes and prices never leave your register.
