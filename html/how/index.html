HOW IT WORKS

Three photos, one tap. Your knife gets a birth-card record.

The three shots

Box + kit + birth card — together

Everything in one frame: the box (label visible), the birth card face-up and readable, the knife, pouch and papers. The card is the star — this is the photo the decoder reads.

The knife, open

Blade fully deployed, whole knife in frame, sharp focus. Show the blade grind and any inlay or graphic.

The knife, closed

Closed, showing the handle face — clip side is best. This is usually your hero shot for the register.

A good card photo

  • Flat, even light — window light beats lamp light
  • Card fills a good chunk of the frame and every printed line is readable
  • Box label (SKU sticker) visible in the same shot
  • Glare across the card — tilt it until the shine is gone
  • No card? That's fine — say so in the note and the box label carries the date

The process

  1. Sign in

    Email address → tap the link we send you. No passwords.

  2. ADD A KNIFE

    You get a tag (K01, K02 …). One knife at a time, start to finish.

  3. Add the three photos

    Take them now or pick from your camera roll. Photo 1 is the card shot.

  4. Note anything the card can't say

    Optional: where you got it, "no card", left-handed, a special edition story.

  5. Hit PROCESS ⚡

    ~15–30 seconds. It reads the birth card and box label and fills in the record — steel, date, inlay, the lot.

  6. Check the card, then SAVE TO REGISTER

    Not right? Save anyway — every field is editable in your register.

  7. Yours to control

    Each knife has a public toggle, sale status, and a delete. Your private notes and prices never leave your register.
