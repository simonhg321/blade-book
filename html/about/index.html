ABOUT BLADE-BOOK

A register for knife collectors. One photo, one card, one page per knife.

WHAT IT IS

  • A register: you photograph the birth card (or a blank one you fill in), blade-book reads it and files the knife under your handle.
  • A public page per knife you choose to show, and one for your whole collection.
  • One search across every public register, so a collector can find the piece they're after.
  • Wants and a board: say what you're looking for, list what you'd trade or sell, and blade-book introduces the two of you by email.

WHAT IT ISN'T

  • Not a certificate of authenticity. A page here proves someone photographed a card — not that the knife is genuine, or what it's worth.
  • Not a marketplace. We never handle money, never take a cut, and never stand between buyer and seller.
  • Not an oracle. The card is read by a model and corrected by the owner. Every field is editable; the owner is the authority.
