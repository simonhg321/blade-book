TERMS

Plain English. If something here surprises you, email us.

A record, not a certificate

blade-book is a collector's register. A page here proves that someone photographed a birth card and filed it; it does not prove that a knife is genuine, where it has been, or what it is worth. Do your own checking before you buy.

No money

We don't sell knives, take payment for them, or stand between buyer and seller. Wants, intros and the board pass an email address along, with both people's consent. The deal, the shipping and the trust are between you.

Your photos stay yours

You keep every right to your photos and your records. You give blade-book permission to show them on your public page and in the search index for as long as the knife is public. Make a knife private, or delete it, and that permission ends with it.

Takedown

If a page shows something that is yours and shouldn't be here, email us with the link. We act within 24 hours.

Delete is real

Deleting your account removes your knives, photos, wants, intros and your public page immediately, not on a schedule. We keep one thing: a one-way hash of your email address, so a new account on the same address doesn't restart the free tier.

Your data

You can download everything you've put here — original photos, every record, private notes and prices included — as one ZIP, any time, subscribed or not. It's yours.

What we store

Your email address, your handle, the knife records and photos you add, the notes you write, and a sign-in cookie. Nothing else. No ads, no trackers, no analytics scripts.

Early access

blade-book is new. Prices and features may change while we find our feet; when they do, about says so first. Questions: email us.
