OTHER MAKERS

Can I use blade-book for knives that aren't Chris Reeve?

Yes. Any knife, any maker, customs included. blade-book started with Chris Reeve because their birth card is the best-documented card in the hobby, and the reader knows that card and box label inside out. Everything else about the register is maker-neutral: the tag, the photos, the public page, search, wants and the board.

For another maker, shoot the knife the same way: the maker's card or certificate, the box, the knife, all in one frame. The reader will transcribe whatever card is in the photo and fill in what it can. Fields that only exist for Chris Reeve, like Sebenza generation or the box SKU, simply stay empty.

My maker doesn't issue a card. Now what?

Print a blank birth card, fill it in by hand with what you know, and shoot it with the knife. The reader treats it like any other card. Every field on it is optional, and you are the source. That is how a custom, an estate find, or a knife that lost its paperwork gets a page.

Will other makers get the same deep reading Chris Reeve gets?

That's the plan. Each maker is one module on our side: what their cards say, what their boxes say, their steels and eras. Tell us which maker you collect. The ones people ask for get built first.

THE CARD

What is a birth card?

The card a maker ships with the knife. For Chris Reeve it names the model, the blade steel and hardness, the inlay or graphic, the hand, and the date the knife was born. There is no serial number on a Chris Reeve knife. The card is the record, which is why blade-book is built around it.

How accurate is the reader?

On printed cards it gets nearly every field right. Handwritten dates are the weak spot. It reads the card, the box label and your note, marks each field high, medium or low confidence, and tells you in two sentences what it identified and what it guessed. Check the card before you save. Every field stays editable after.

What makes a good card photo?
  • Flat, even light. Window light beats lamp light.
  • The card fills a good chunk of the frame and every printed line is readable.
  • The box label is in the same shot.
  • Tilt until the glare is off the card.

One flat-lay is enough. Two more shots, knife open and knife closed, make a prettier page. The full walkthrough is on how it works.

Does a page on blade-book prove my knife is genuine?

No. A page proves that someone photographed a card and filed it. It does not prove the knife is real, where it has been, or what it is worth. Do your own checking before you buy.

How long does processing take, and what does it cost me?

About fifteen to thirty seconds. Processing is included; there is no per-knife charge.

YOUR REGISTER

What are the K-numbers?

Your tags. The first knife you add is K01, the next K02, and so on. They are yours, in the order you filed them. One knife at a time, start to finish, so the numbers never skip.

What is public and what is private?

Every knife starts private. Flip its public toggle to show it: a public knife shows its cover photo and the card fields on your public page and in search. Listing a knife for sale or trade makes it public, since the board and wants only see public knives. Your private notes and any prices you record never leave your register, public knife or not. A private knife shows nothing anywhere.

Can I fix what the reader got wrong?

Yes, every field, any time. The reader fills in the record; you are the authority.

Can I take my data out?

Any time, subscribed or not. Settings gives you one ZIP with your original photos, every record as CSV and JSON, thumbnails, your private notes and prices, and a printable register page you can keep on paper.

Is delete real?

Yes. Deleting a knife removes it immediately. Deleting your account removes your knives, photos, wants, intros and public page, immediately, not on a schedule. The one thing we keep is a one-way hash of your email so a new account on the same address doesn't restart the free tier.

WANTS AND THE BOARD

How do wants work?

Say what you're looking for. When a knife someone lists for sale or trade matches, blade-book emails an introduction, once per person and knife, ever. Both people's email addresses are passed along only with both people's consent. What happens next is between the two of you.

What is the board?

A list of knives their owners would trade or sell. To post, your email must be verified and the knife must have been on your register, live, for at least a week. That week is deliberate. Contact goes to the seller first.

Does blade-book handle the money?

No. We never sell knives, take payment for them, take a cut, or stand between buyer and seller. The deal, the shipping and the trust are yours.

PRICE

What does it cost?

Nothing right now. blade-book is free during early access: add as many knives as you like, any age, any maker, and use every search filter.

When there are enough collectors here to make it worth running properly, around fifty, we will talk about a small subscription (what we have in mind: see the sign-in page). You will hear it from us first, with plenty of notice. There is no checkout and no card on file. Questions: email us.

Whatever happens, export and delete are yours today and always.

PRIVACY

What do you store?

Your email address, your handle, the records and photos you add, the notes you write, and a sign-in cookie. No ads, no trackers, no analytics scripts.

Do my photos give away where I live?

Photos on public pages are stripped of camera metadata, including location, before they are served. Your originals in your register keep their metadata, and they are in your export ZIP untouched.

Someone posted my knife or my photo.

Email us the link. We act within 24 hours. The whole policy in plain English is on terms.

SIGNING IN

Where is my password?

There isn't one. Enter your email and we send a link. It works once and expires in fifteen minutes. Tap it on the same phone or computer you asked from and you're in.

I opened the link on a different device and got a confirm page.

That's expected. When the link is opened somewhere other than where it was requested, we ask you to confirm before signing you in, so a forwarded link can't sign in someone else. Confirm, and you're in.

The link never came.

Give it a minute and check spam. The sender is noreply at blade-book.com. Still nothing? Request another.
